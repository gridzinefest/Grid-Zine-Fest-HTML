How drop day works

  • 09:30 Line starts at the door on Calle Garmendia. Water and shade provided.
  • 10:00 Site and doors open together. One pair per style, per person.
  • 10:15 Bags in the online checkout are held for 15 minutes, then released.
  • 14:00 Last call for Monday shipping anywhere in Mexico.
See you at 10

Before you set an alarm

Can I reserve a pair before Saturday?

No holds before a drop. Everyone gets the same 10:00 start, online and at the door.

How many pairs can I buy?

One pair per style per person on drop day. Hoodies and caps have no limit.

What time is 10:00 for me?

Hermosillo time, which stays on UTC−7 all year. The countdown on our home page already does the maths for you.

Do you ship drop-day orders right away?

Orders placed by 14:00 on Saturday go out Monday morning with tracking, anywhere in Mexico.

Can’t wait for Saturday?

Sixteen pieces are live right now.
